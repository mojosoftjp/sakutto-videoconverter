Every quick video fix, in one app.

Four everyday tasks — just switch tabs.

Compress to a target size

Just pick the size that fits where you're sending it.

  • Discord (10 MB, legacy 8 MB), e-mail attachment (25 MB), X (512 MB), or any size in MB
  • Re-encodes once automatically if the result is still too large
  • Caps the resolution automatically when the bitrate would be too low

Compress by quality

Pick a quality (CRF) and make files smaller.

  • H.264 (compatible) or H.265 (high compression)
  • Maximum resolution (1080p, 720p, 480p)
  • Audio bitrate (192, 128, 64 kbps)

Convert formats

Convert to MP4, WebM, AVI, MKV, MOV or GIF — many files at once.

  • Quality (High Quality, Balanced, Small Size)
  • Resolution (4K, 1080p, 720p, 480p) and frame rate (60, 30, 24 fps)
  • Audio: copy, AAC, or none (remove audio)

Trim

Cut out just the part you need while watching the preview.

  • Lossless copy (fast, original quality)
  • Re-encode (exact cut)
  • If lossless copy would change the length, the app shows the real length before it starts

Extract audio

Save just the audio track of a video.

  • MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG
  • Bitrate (128–320 kbps) and sample rate (44.1 or 48 kHz)
  • As many files as you like in one go

Install and go

FFmpeg, the engine that processes the videos, is built into the app.

  • No other software to install
  • A progress bar, and a button to stop midway
  • If compression fails, the reason is shown

Frequently asked questions

Can it make a video small enough for Discord (10 MB)?

Yes. In the Compress tab, set the target size to "Discord (10MB)". The app calculates the bitrate from the length of the video and compresses it to fit in 10 MB. If the result is still too large, it re-encodes once automatically.

Can it process many videos at once?

Yes. Convert, Audio and Compress process all the files you add, one after another, automatically.

Are my original videos kept?

Yes. Results are always saved as new files. If a file with the same name exists, a number is added, so nothing is overwritten.

Can I turn iPhone videos (MOV) into MP4?

Yes. Choose MP4 (H.264) in the Convert tab to get an MP4 that plays on most devices.

Can I pull just the audio out of a video?

Yes. The Audio tab saves MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC or OGG, for as many files as you like.

Can I trim without losing quality?

Yes. "Lossless copy" in the Trim tab cuts quickly and keeps the original quality. It cuts on key frames, so if the length would change, the app tells you before it starts and lets you switch to re-encode for an exact cut.

Does video processing work without an internet connection?

Yes. Converting and compressing happen on your PC with the FFmpeg built into the app.

Is it a one-time purchase?

Yes. Buy it once on the Microsoft Store and keep using it with no monthly fee.
